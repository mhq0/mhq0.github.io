Attempts and ads

Each level starts with 10 independent attempts. A fall or hazard uses one. Exhausting available attempts locks play for two hours before a free refill. A verified purchase or an earned ad reward unlocks play. Ads require internet access and availability from Google; closing before the reward is earned grants no attempt.

Motion and language

Hold your phone comfortably and calibrate tilt. Touch control is also available. Use the language button to switch between English and Arabic.

Purchases and device data

Purchases are processed by Apple. Attempts are delivered only after Apple confirms the transaction. Game progress and consumable balances are device-local and are not automatically restored after deleting app data. For Apple billing and refund requests, use Apple's purchase support.
